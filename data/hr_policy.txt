CHAPTER 2: WORK HOURS, SCHEDULES & ATTENDANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

2.1 CORE OPERATING HOURS
- Standard Working Hours: The official workweek at AGT runs from Monday through Friday. Standard business hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM local time, with a mandatory 1-hour unpaid lunch break between 12:00 PM and 2:00 PM.
- Core Hours Mandate: Regardless of flexible schedules, all full-time employees must be available online or in-office during Core Hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
- Total Weekly Requirement: Full-time staff are expected to work 40 hours per week.

2.2 WORK MODELS (ON-SITE, HYBRID, AND REMOTE)
1. On-Site Employees: Assigned to physical offices (Headquarters or regional hubs). Attendance must be logged using the badge reader system upon arrival and departure.
2. Hybrid Employees: Required to work in-office a minimum of 3 designated days per week (typically Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday). Hybrid arrangements must be documented in writing and approved by the Department Head.
3. Fully Remote Employees: Permitted to work from an approved home location. Remote employees must maintain a stable high-speed internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps download speed) and remain reachable via Slack and email during Core Hours.

2.3 ATTENDANCE, PUNCTUALITY, AND OVERTIME
- Tardiness: An employee is considered late if they fail to log in or arrive within 15 minutes of their agreed start time without prior notification to their line manager. Three unexcused tardy incidents within a 30-day rolling window will trigger an informal verbal warning from HR.
- Overtime Policy: Overtime work (exceeding 40 hours in a single calendar week) applies strictly to Non-Exempt employees. All overtime must be pre-approved by the CFO (Marcus Vance) and line manager in writing. Pre-approved overtime is paid at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER 3: LEAVE POLICIES & TIME OFF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

3.1 PAID TIME OFF (PTO) / VACATION LEAVE
- Annual Accrual Rate: Full-time employees accrue Paid Time Off at a rate of 1.5 days per completed calendar month of service, totaling 18 days of PTO per calendar year.
- Accrual Start: New hires begin accruing PTO from their first full day of employment but cannot take accrued PTO during their 90-day probationary period, except in emergencies approved by Sarah Jenkins.
- Notice Requirement: PTO requests of 3 consecutive days or fewer must be submitted at least 5 business days in advance via the HR Portal. PTO requests exceeding 3 consecutive days require 14 days' advance notice and manager approval.
- Rollover & Cap: A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the new year, or they will lapse without cash payout.

3.2 SICK LEAVE POLICY
- Annual Entitlement: All full-time employees receive 10 paid Sick Leave days per calendar year, granted upfront on January 1st. Mid-year hires receive a pro-rated allocation.
- Medical Certification:
  * For sick leaves lasting 1 or 2 consecutive days, self-certification via the HR Portal is acceptable.
  * For sick leave extending to 3 or more consecutive business days, a valid medical certificate issued by a licensed physician must be uploaded to the portal before returning to work.
- Unused Sick Leave: Unused sick leave cannot be cashed out upon termination or carried forward into the next calendar year.

3.3 PARENTAL LEAVE
- Maternity Leave: Female employees who have completed at least 12 months of continuous service are eligible for 26 weeks of paid Maternity Leave for up to two surviving children. Requests must be submitted 60 days prior to the expected delivery date.
- Paternity / Partner Leave: Non-birthing parents who have completed at least 12 months of continuous service are entitled to 4 weeks of paid Paternity Leave, which must be used within 6 months of the child’s birth or legal adoption.

3.4 BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Employees are entitled to paid bereavement leave based on relationship proximity:
- Immediate Family (Spouse, Child, Parent, Sibling): Up to 5 consecutive paid working days.
- Extended Family (Grandparent, In-law, Aunt, Uncle): Up to 2 consecutive paid working days.
Requests must be routed to Ananya Patel in HR.

3.5 CASUAL & PERSONAL EMERGENCY LEAVE
Employees are allocated 4 days of Unpaid Casual Leave per year for unplanned personal administrative emergencies that do not fall under medical reasons. Prior approval from the line manager is required where possible.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER 4: CODE OF CONDUCT & RULES & REGULATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

4.1 PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT & ANTI-HARASSMENT
AGT enforces a strict Zero-Tolerance Policy regarding harassment, discrimination, bullying, or retaliation based on race, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, religion, or national origin.
- Reporting Incident Procedure: Employees witnessing or experiencing workplace misconduct must submit a formal complaint directly to Sarah Jenkins or use the anonymous reporting portal at ethics@apexglobal.tech.
- Investigation Timeline: HR initiates investigations within 48 hours of complaint receipt. Findings are reviewed by a committee comprising Sarah Jenkins (VP of HR) and Dr. Aris Thorne (CEO). Violations result in disciplinary action up to immediate termination without notice.

4.2 INFORMATION SECURITY & ACCEPTABLE USE
- Device Usage: All software development, corporate communication, and data handling must occur on AGT-issued devices. Using personal unencrypted devices for corporate work is prohibited without written authorization from CTO Elena Rostova.
- Password and Credential Rules:
  * Passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters and combine letters, numbers, and symbols.
  * Passwords must be updated every 90 days.
  * Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is compulsory across all company applications (Slack, Google Workspace, AWS, GitHub).
- Data Classification: Corporate data is classified as Public, Internal, or Strictly Confidential. Proprietary source code, customer data, and employee PII are classified as Strictly Confidential and must never be copied, transferred, or stored on local drives outside approved encrypted repositories.

4.3 SOCIAL MEDIA & EXTERNAL COMMUNICATIONS
- Employees must not speak to media representatives or issue public statements on behalf of Apex Global Technologies Inc. without written clearance from the Marketing & Communications Director.
- Employees sharing work-related milestones on personal social accounts (e.g., LinkedIn) must include a disclaimer stating: "Views expressed are my own and do not represent AGT."

4.4 CONFLICT OF INTEREST & SECONDARY EMPLOYMENT
- Full-time employees are prohibited from undertaking secondary employment, freelance work, or advisory roles with companies in competing industries without prior written permission from CEO Dr. Aris Thorne.
- Personal relationships between supervisors and direct reports must be disclosed immediately to HR to prevent conflicts of interest in performance evaluations.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER 5: PERFORMANCE EVALUATIONS & PROMOTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1 REVIEW CYCLES
- Bi-Annual Reviews: Performance evaluations occur twice yearly:
  1. Mid-Year Review: July (Focuses on goal alignment and feedback).
  2. End-of-Year Review: December (Determines performance ratings, merit increases, and bonus distributions).
- Evaluation Framework: Employees are evaluated on a 1 to 5 scale across key competencies: Technical Mastery, Collaboration, Ownership, and Value Delivery.

5.2 PROBATIONARY PERIOD
- All new hires undergo a 90-day Probationary Period.
- A formal 45-day check-in and 90-day final review are conducted by the immediate manager and HR.
- Successful completion leads to permanent status confirmation; underperformance during this window may result in probation extension (up to 30 days) or immediate contract termination.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER 6: COMPENSATION, BENEFITS & EXPENSES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1 PAYROLL & PAY SCHEDULES
- Pay Period: Wages are paid on a monthly schedule on the last working day of each calendar month.
- Direct Deposit: Salary payouts are processed electronically into designated employee bank accounts registered with Marcus Vance's finance team.

6.2 HEALTH INSURANCE & WELLNESS PROGRAM
- Health Coverage: Full-time staff are eligible for AGT's Comprehensive Health Plan starting on their first calendar day of the month following their hire date.
- Wellness Allowance: Employees receive a $100 USD (or equivalent local currency) monthly stipend usable for gym memberships, mental health counseling apps, or ergonomic office supplies. Reimbursements must be submitted through the Finance Portal by the 20th of each month with valid receipts.

6.3 TRAVEL & EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
- Business Travel: Travel for business purposes must be pre-approved by CFO Marcus Vance.
- Meal Allowances:
  * Daily Meal Cap during domestic travel: $75 per day.
  * Daily Meal Cap during international travel: $120 per day.
- Submission Deadline: Expense reports submitted more than 30 days after the expense occurred will be automatically rejected.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER 7: SEPARATION & TERMINATION POLICIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7.1 VOLUNTARY RESIGNATION
- Notice Periods:
  * Individual Contributors: Required to provide 30 days' written notice.
  * Managers, Directors, and Executives: Required to provide 60 days' written notice.
- Resignation letters must be submitted simultaneously to the line manager and Sarah Jenkins (VP of HR).

7.2 EXIT PROCESS & FINAL SETTLEMENT
- Asset Return: Upon resignation or termination, all company property—including laptops, monitors, security keycards, and intellectual assets—must be returned to Kevin Zhang in HR by or before the employee's last working day.
- Final Settlement (F&F): Full and final financial settlements, including earned unpaid salary and eligible accrued PTO payouts, will be processed within 14 calendar days following the final working date.
